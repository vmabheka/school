How automatic receipts work

  • When a payment is recorded, the receipt is sent automatically to the student's parent(s) on the channels you enabled above.
  • Parents can register by WhatsApp: text REGISTER <child name> <level> (e.g. REGISTER Tanaka Moyo Grade 6) and follow the prompts to add their own details.
  • Registered parents can request their records any time: BALANCE, RESULTS, RECEIPTS, RECORD, HELP.
  • Receipts can be re-sent manually from the receipt page.
  • If a channel is not configured, the message is logged in the sync log instead.
